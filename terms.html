What the app does

V-Taper AI estimates your shoulder-to-waist proportion from a body photo on your device, tracks change over time, and provides meal logging. If you choose AI Meal Scan, a meal photo is sent to our processing service and Google Gemini to return an editable nutrition estimate.

Not medical advice

Measurements are estimates derived from photos and vary with how a photo is taken. The app provides fitness information for general interest only. It is not a medical device and provides no medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise.

Subscriptions

An auto-renewing subscription (weekly or yearly) is required after onboarding to access results, trends, nutrition, and additional scans. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. No free trial is currently offered. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. Standard Apple EULA applies.
