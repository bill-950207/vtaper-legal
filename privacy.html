Privacy Policy

V-Taper AI: Get V-Shaped · Last updated August 11, 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your phone. All body measurement happens on your device. We run no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Photos and measurements

Photos you take or select are processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning. Neither the photo, the body cutout, nor any measurement derived from it is ever uploaded, transmitted, or shared. Measurements and cutout images are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and are deleted when you use "Delete all scans" or uninstall the app.

Body details you enter

Height, weight, and age are optional, stored only on your device, and used only to display context next to your measurements.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. To activate your subscription across reinstalls we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier generated for your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal detail — we never collect those. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing else. No account, no email, no location, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics.

Reference data

Percentiles shown in the app are computed on your device against ANSUR II, a public anthropometric survey. Your data is never added to it.
