Privacy Policy

V-Taper AI: Get V-Shaped · Last updated August 12, 2026

The short version

Body scans are measured on your device and are never uploaded. A meal photo is transmitted only when you explicitly choose AI Meal Scan. We run no user accounts, advertising, or cross-app tracking.

Body scans and measurements

Photos you take or select are processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning. Neither the photo, the body cutout, nor any measurement derived from it is ever uploaded, transmitted, or shared. Measurements and cutout images are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and are deleted when you use "Delete all local data" or uninstall the app.

AI Meal Scan

When you choose AI Meal Scan, the selected meal photo is sent over HTTPS to our processing service hosted by Vercel and then to Google's Gemini service to estimate visible foods, calories, and macronutrients. We use the photo only to return that estimate. Our application does not intentionally retain the photo after the request finishes. Vercel and Google may process request data under their service and security terms. Do not submit a photo containing people, sensitive documents, or information unrelated to the meal. You can instead add every meal manually without sending a photo.

If you save a meal with its photo, that copy and its nutrition values remain in the app's private storage on your phone until you delete the meal, use "Delete all local data," or uninstall the app.

Body details you enter

Height, weight, and age are optional and stored only on your device. They are used to display measurement context and, when you request it, calculate a starting nutrition target. They are not sent with an AI Meal Scan.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. To activate your subscription across reinstalls we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier generated for your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal detail — we never collect those. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Diagnostics and data we do not collect

Frameworks required to operate purchases and on-device machine learning may process limited purchase, product-interaction, performance, or diagnostic data. We do not use this data for advertising or tracking. We do not request an account, email address, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Your controls

You can delete individual scans and meals. "Delete all local data" removes all scans, cutouts, body details, nutrition targets, meals, and saved meal photos from the app. Deleting the app also removes its local container. Apple device backups may separately retain app data according to your iCloud or computer backup settings.

Reference data

Percentiles shown in the app are computed on your device against ANSUR II, a public anthropometric survey. Your data is never added to it.
